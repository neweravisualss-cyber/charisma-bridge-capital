Frequently Asked Questions

Here are some useful things to know before making a financing enquiry.

How do I apply for a loan?

Complete the enquiry form on this website. When you submit it, WhatsApp will open with your details ready to send to our team.

What types of loans do you offer?

Our listed categories include small business and vendor financing, personal loans, SSB loans and asset finance. Contact our team to discuss availability and eligibility.

How much can I apply for?

You can indicate the amount you need in the application form. The amount available will depend on the applicable assessment and approval process.

How long does the application process take?

Processing times depend on the information provided and the company's review process. Contact our team for an update on your enquiry.

Do I need collateral?

Requirements depend on the financing product and assessment. Please confirm the applicable requirements with our team before applying.

How can I contact Charisma Bridge Capital?

Contact us on WhatsApp at +263 77 493 6425 .
